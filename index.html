Chickens and Turkeys give you plump bird meat when plucked. Rathskeller Fork usually has lot of chickens to kill.
Your camp dog is the easiest way to get this, or try Armadilo by the undertakers, and maybe Tumbleweed around the butchers.
The western end of Big Valley where the two roads meet is a good hunting location. There should be plenty just south of Butcher Creek and Elysian Pool, and also around Rhodes, and there's often some in the triangle of railway lines just north of Emerald Station.
Sell at any butchers, ones at Blackwater and Rhodes are very close to the fast travel posts. Birds and small animals from your satchel are suitable, fish are not though.
